About Us

Three generations. Same shop.

Newark Body Shop opened on Johnson Street in 1955 and never left. Three generations of the same family have run the place — cars came in with chrome bumpers, then with composite plastics, then with cameras and sensors in every panel. The metalwork didn't change. We just kept up.

Whether you got rear-ended on Route 71, slid into a deer in Big Grove, or your hail-pitted roof needs PDR, we'll work directly with your insurance company and get you back on the road without the back-and-forth.

What We Do

Services.

Body, frame, paint, plastic. Anything that bends, breaks, scratches, or rusts — we fix it.

01

Collision Repair

Fender-benders to total reconstructions. We pull panels, replace what can't be saved, and blend paint so the repair line disappears.

02

Frame Straightening

Hard hits bend more than sheet metal. We measure, pull, and re-align the frame so the car drives straight again.

03

Paint & Refinish

Computer-matched paint, panel-blended for an invisible repair. Clear coat, baked finish, dust-controlled booth.

04

Hail & Dent Repair

Paintless dent removal where the paint's intact. Saves the original finish and a lot of money on the insurance claim.

05

Glass Replacement

Windshields and side glass replaced and calibrated — modern cars need camera recalibration after a windshield, and we handle it.

06

Rust Repair

Cut out the rust, weld in fresh metal, prime and seal. Done right, it won't come back through the paint two winters later.

How It Works

From dent to drive-off.

STEP 01

Estimate

Bring the car in, we write a free estimate. Photos and itemized parts list go straight to your insurance.

STEP 02

Approval

We coordinate with the adjuster, finalize the scope, and order parts. You get a delivery date in writing.

STEP 03

Repair

Panel work, frame pulls, paint and refinish in our booth. Status updates by phone or text every few days.

STEP 04

Drive Off

Final inspection, wash, and your car comes home. Lifetime warranty on workmanship comes with every job.
